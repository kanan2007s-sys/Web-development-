About CHARUSAT

Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT) is a premier institution committed to excellence in higher education, research, innovation, and community development. Established with the vision of transforming society through quality education, the university offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across disciplines including engineering, technology, pharmacy, management, computer applications, sciences, nursing, and allied health sciences. At CHARUSAT, students benefit from an industry-oriented curriculum, experienced faculty, modern laboratories, advanced research facilities, and a vibrant campus environment that encourages academic excellence and personal growth. The university emphasizes innovation, entrepreneurship, ethical values, and lifelong learning, preparing graduates to excel in their careers and contribute meaningfully to society. Through strong industry collaborations, research initiatives, and global partnerships, CHARUSAT continues to empower students with the knowledge, skills, and confidence needed to succeed in an ever-evolving world.
